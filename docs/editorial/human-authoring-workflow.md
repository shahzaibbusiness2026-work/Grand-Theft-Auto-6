# Taish Gaming: human author + SEO/editorial partner

## Working agreement

The site owner writes the core article and provides their own experience, reasoning and voice. Codex helps research, verify sources, structure the article, edit the draft, implement technical SEO and assess results. The final text and byline accurately reflect those contributions. A complete AI-written draft should be described as AI-assisted/co-written rather than represented as wholly human-written.

We distinguish the author's direct observations, primary-source reporting, interpretation and unknowns. We use the author's supplied name or an accurate collective byline; we do not invent credentials, testing, interviews or personal experiences. The human author reviews the edited article and confirms the final text before publication.

## One article at a time

1. Choose a reader question using available query/page data and the site's actual strengths. Check whether an existing URL already answers it.
2. Codex prepares a short brief: the reader's question, useful sections, primary sources, claims that need evidence, related site links and uncertainties.
3. The author writes a rough draft in their own words. A full spoken narration can also be transcribed and edited. Rough grammar is fine; original reasoning and accurate facts matter more than polish.
4. Codex checks facts, dates, links, attribution and any measurements, then edits for clarity while retaining the author's voice. Substantial suggested additions remain visible as editorial contributions for review.
5. The author checks that the final version says what they mean. We record what was personally tested and what came from reporting.
6. Codex prepares an accurate title, description, headings, internal links, image alternatives and structured data that matches the visible page. Then we publish the approved article and check the live result.
7. When enough data is available, review query/page impressions and clicks. Compare meaningful date ranges and record changes rather than reacting to every daily fluctuation.

## SEO, AEO, GEO and AIO in practice

- SEO: useful indexable pages, accurate titles, valid canonicals, working links, meaningful navigation, readable mobile pages and reliable content.
- AEO: answer the reader's main question clearly near the start, then explain the reasoning, evidence and limitations. Use question headings or a table only where they improve comprehension.
- GEO/AIO: provide original observations, precise source links, clear entities, dates and methods that another system can reference accurately. Measure citations separately from referral clicks.

Google says its existing SEO fundamentals apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode; no special AI file or special schema is required. Bing's AI Performance reporting measures citations on supported Microsoft experiences, not all AI services and not necessarily traffic. These practices improve the content and its eligibility; they do not guarantee an AI citation or a search ranking.

Sources: [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), [Google's generative AI content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content), [Bing AI Performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c).

## Information to send with a draft

- Your draft or complete narration, preferred byline and intended reader.
- The question you want the article to answer and the point you want to make.
- Source links and which facts came from each source.
- Your own screenshots or test notes, if available. For tests: platform, game version/date, conditions and method.
- Anything you are unsure about or want checked.

We will improve existing URLs when that best serves the reader, keep author attribution accurate, and leave unsupported details unknown until evidence changes.
