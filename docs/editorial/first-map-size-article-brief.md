**Reader question:** Can I trust the GTA 6 map-size numbers I see online, and what is actually supported by official material?

This is a reported explainer unless you have genuine first-hand access and documented measurements. Human authorship does not require pretending to have played an unreleased game.

- Any later official announcement that directly supplies a map measurement. Check the source again before publication rather than relying on this brief's date.

## Write these sections in your own words

1. **The answer first.** Explain what the sources you reviewed establish and which map-size claim they do not establish. Keep the scope tied to that review.
2. **What Rockstar shows about Leonida.** Explain the setting and named destinations with direct links. Separate official names from a fan's reconstructed geography.
3. **Why km² estimates can differ.** Explain the importance of the land/water boundary, scale, perspective and measurement method. Use a documented example only if you can trace it to its original author and method.
4. **What a GTA V comparison needs.** Explain why both maps need the same area definition and measurement method. Do not fill in an unsupported ratio for the sake of a comparison table.
5. **What would change the answer.** Identify the primary evidence or reproducible in-game measurement needed to update the article.
